Research profile

About

My research covers robotic mechanism design, theoretical kinematics using screw theory, Lie groups and Lie algebra, parallel and reconfigurable manipulators, continuum robots, mechanism synthesis, physical simulation, machine learning, intelligent manipulation, physics-informed learning, and quantum computation for robotics. The objective is to combine rigorous mathematical analysis with experimentally relevant robotic systems and emerging computational methods.
